Articulation

Three authored states. The voice travels Onset → Continuant while held, and Continuant → Termination on release. Authority blends the drawn path with spring physics; temperature makes the physics wander.

Playing

Layer A

Pulsar emitters. With Formant 1, Cycles 1 and a rectangular window it is a plain band-limited oscillator; raise Formant or narrow the window and it becomes pulses with a formant.

Layer B

A second layer for detuned beating, ring mod, FM into A, or hard sync to A.

Voice character

Mixer, digital resolution, resonator and analog drift. Ring is set per state.

Filter and VCA

Runs at twice the sample rate with the folder, so drive and resonance stay clean at the top. Cutoff is set per state.

Schaeffer macros

Mass moves formants, filter and vocoder bands together. Grain trades smooth tone for particles. Allure adds motion everywhere.

Material vocoder

Body is the material that gets reshaped. Skin is the sound whose spectral motion is pressed onto it. Fixed skin uses only the band levels, which is the GRAINS move.

Feedback path

Delay, filter and saturator inside the loop. Each state sets how much of the voice is sent in.

Space
